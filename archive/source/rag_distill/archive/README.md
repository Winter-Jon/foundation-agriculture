# Archived Round Builders

Round-numbered builders through Round 114 are retained here as immutable historical procedure evidence. They do not form the active sampling interface.

These files are archive-only historical source. They are not imported by the
installed package and are not selected by any current `agrinet` workflow.

For the current route, see [`docs/source-map.md`](../../../../docs/source-map.md)
and the supported implementation under `src/agrinet/research/hcv/`. The
historical builders may refer to paths and assumptions from their original
execution period; inspect them for provenance rather than treating them as
current launchers.
