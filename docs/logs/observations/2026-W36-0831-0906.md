# Observations — 2026-W36 (0831–0906)

## 2026-09-03 02:27:43 CST - Verify formal OpenAgri v2 class and image balance

Tags: open-agri-v2, dataset-distribution, class-balance, long-tail

### Context

Recheck whether the formal v2 Known/Unknown and disease/pest proportions are
reasonable for paper-facing use, using the current formal manifests rather than
intermediate split summaries.

### Details

- Read only `datasets/AgriNet-1K/open_agri_v2/manifests/class_split.jsonl` and
  `manifests/images.jsonl`; no dataset content was modified.
- Computed role/domain split counts, test images per class, final Known
  train-candidate quantiles and Gini coefficients, dev/test/reference coverage,
  and long-tail/confusability composition.

### Result

- Class roles are balanced within domain: disease 73 Known / 72 Unknown and
  pest 36 Known / 36 Unknown. Test role mass is 534 Known / 485 Unknown
  (52.4% / 47.6%); domain mass is 547 disease / 472 pest (53.7% / 46.3%).
- Test is class-complete and intentionally small: disease averages 3.77 test
  images/class and pest 6.56. This difference comes from the frozen source
  inventory, not a role-selection use of test data.
- Known training images are almost equal in total across domains (69,907
  disease / 71,575 pest) but are strongly long-tailed by class. Disease Known:
  median 459, range 25--6,252, Gini 0.627, seven classes below 50 candidates.
  Pest Known: median 1,395, range 124--6,134, Gini 0.470, no class below 100.
- Unknown covers the intended hard/resource-limited strata: 65.3% of disease
  Unknown and 63.9% of pest Unknown are long-tail; 50.0% and 52.8%, respectively,
  are visually confusable by the frozen neighbour proxy.

### Conclusion

- Verified: class and final-test role proportions are reasonable and close to
  balanced for a class-disjoint benchmark.
- Verified: the remaining image-level imbalance is material and intentional;
  it preserves the agricultural long tail, but invalidates a standalone
  image-micro interpretation.
- Recommendation: use domain/class-balanced SFT sampling and class-macro
  accuracy as the primary result, with micro accuracy and all declared slices
  reported secondarily. Do not remove the seven low-resource Known disease
  classes; disclose them as a fixed low-resource slice.

Apply the reporting/sampling policy in future formal-v2 training and evaluation
configs; do not alter roles or frozen test inventory merely to equalize images.
