### Acceptance
- Preflight has complete image-isolation and public-evidence-lineage audits, no
  protocol violation or internal-ID/target leakage, and a demonstrable
  non-duplicate second-turn evidence delta in the predefined recall-repair
  cells.
- SFT is reproducible from a frozen manifest and token-balanced composition.
- Smoke and 618 results have exact unique manifest coverage, zero explicit
  errors, zero malformed/invalid tool calls, zero final tool calls, zero
  unparseable answers, and zero terminal-closure failures.
- The paired analysis uses the predefined matched raw baseline and verifies
  Direct retention as well as the RAG effect; a small unpaired aggregate gain
  is insufficient for promotion.

### Risks
- The preflight can show that available public retrieval evidence lacks a
  meaningful recall-repair signal; that blocks SFT rather than justifying a
  speculative training run.
- Existing 618 and SFT image exposure must be checked by hash, not path alone.
- The managed Lite database must be exercised through HTTP while the service is
  running; opening its DB file in a second client is unsafe.
